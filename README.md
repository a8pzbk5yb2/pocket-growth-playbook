# Pocket Growth Playbook

A clean, modern 2x4 grid UI/UX mobile app showcase of a fintech and investment application named "PowerBank" in a vibrant emerald green and crisp white color scheme. Eight rounded smartphone screens arranged neatly in two rows of four on a white background. **Top Row:**

1. **Home Screen:** Green header with lightning bolt logo "PowerBank", welcome text "Welcome back, Riyaj Khan", green gradient balance card showing "₹0.00" with 3D wallet and gold coins icon, quick stats for Withdrawable Earnings and Deposit Funds, "+ Add Funds" and "Withdraw" buttons, empty transaction state illustration, and a 6-tab bottom navigation bar (Home, Plans, Task, Team, Wallet, Me).
2. **Plans Screen:** Green banner with 3D red gift box and gold coins reading "Choose Your Plan & Start Earning Today!", filter pills (All Plans, Popular, Premium), vertical cards for Basic Plan (₹100, ₹10/day), Silver Plan (₹500, ₹60/day), Gold Plan (₹1,000, ₹130/day), and Diamond Plan (₹2,000, ₹280/day) with crown and diamond icons and green "Invest Now" pill buttons.
3. **Task Screen:** Green header banner with 3D checklist illustration reading "Complete Tasks & Earn Bonus", category tabs, and a list of daily tasks (Daily Login, Watch Video, Share App, Read News, Complete Survey) with colorful square icons and green "Go" buttons.
4. **My Team Screen:** Green header with 3D avatar group, "My Team Members: 0" counter, 3-column stat row (Total Team, Active Team, Team Income), green referral banner "Invite Your Friends Get ₹50 Bonus", and a "Team Benefits" checklist. **Bottom Row:**
5. **Wallet Screen:** Green header, balance card showing "₹0.00", transaction filter tabs (All, Deposits, Withdrawals), empty state illustration, and action list items for Add Money, Withdraw Money, and Transaction History.
6. **Profile (Me) Screen:** Curved green header with circular user avatar, "Riyaj Khan", "User ID: PB123456", green "Active Member" badge, stats for Total Earnings and Team Members, and a clean settings menu (My Investments, Bank/UPI Details, Contact Support, Change Password, News & Updates, About Us, Logout).
7. **Deposit Funds Screen:** Green header with 3D Rupee coin icon, payment method selector list with icons (UPI Google Pay/PhonePe/Paytm, Bank Transfer, Debit/Credit Card, Wallet), full-width green "Proceed to Pay" button, and "100% Secure & Encrypted" footer badge.
8. **Withdraw Funds Screen:** Green header with 3D wallet and cash icon, "Available Balance ₹0.00", amount input field ("Min. ₹100"), quick-select amount chips (₹100, ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹5,000), bank account dropdown, and green "Request Withdrawal" button. **Style:** Flat vector UI combined with glossy 3D isometric fintech icons, soft card drop shadows, clean sans-serif typography, Indian Rupee (₹) currency formatting, Dribbble and Behance UI design presentation quality, ultra-crisp text, 8k resolution.

 A clean, modern side-by-side UI/UX mobile app showcase featuring two rounded smartphone screens (Login and Sign Up) for a fintech application named "PowerBank", styled in a vibrant emerald green and crisp white color scheme on a light neutral background. Sign Up" with "Sign Up" highlighted in green. Right Screen (Register): A matching white floating card titled "Create Your PowerBank Account" with subtitle "Join today and start your investment journey".
